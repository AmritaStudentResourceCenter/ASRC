Our Mission

At ASRC, we believe every student deserves easy access to well-organized, reliable academic resources. We're building a centralized platform that transforms how students find, share, and learn from lecture notes and campus information.

Started as a simple idea to solve a real problem, ASRC has grown into a student-driven initiative committed to supporting the academic journey of CSE (AI) students at Amrita.

Want to Contribute?

ASRC is built by students like you. Whether fixing a typo, adding lecture notes, or introducing new features, your contributions are welcome!
