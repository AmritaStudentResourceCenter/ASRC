01 // Why We Built This

The night before internals shouldn't be a scavenger hunt.

A familiar problem across every semester, solved with a simple, well-structured index.

We have all been there. It is 11:30 PM the night before a mid-semester exam, and you realize you are missing the lecture slides for Unit 3.

Our professors put immense effort into preparing detailed lecture notes, annotated PDFs, problem sets, and reference handouts. Yet, the way those files reached students was fragmented. Some lived in WhatsApp groups buried under hundreds of messages, others were inside nested Google Drive folders with restricted permissions, and a few were shared via temporary links that expired weeks ago.

Instead of complaining about the chaos, twelve of us decided to sit down, collect every verified document shared by our faculty, standardize the file naming, and build ASRC—a permanent, fast, and searchable home for our coursework.

Before ASRC Scattered
  • ✕ Scrolling through 400+ WhatsApp messages looking for final_notes_v2.pdf
  • ✕ "You need permission to access this Google Drive folder" right before exams
  • ✕ Inconsistent versions between different class sections
With ASRC Centralized
  • ✓ Cleanly indexed by Semester, Course Code, and Module number
  • ✓ Direct, one-click viewing and downloading—no login walls
  • ✓ Verified faculty materials maintained and updated by a 12-member team
02 // Repository Standards

Designed for clarity and speed.

We treated organizing lecture notes like an engineering problem: clean taxonomy, predictable naming, and zero bloat.

01

Faculty-Verified Material

Every slide deck, PDF handout, and question bank in the archive comes directly from official classroom distributions.

SOURCE: OFFICIAL LECTURES
02

Structured Taxonomy

No more mysterious filenames. Resources are strictly categorized by Subject Code, Unit/Module, and Topic.

FORMAT: /SEM/COURSE/UNIT
03

Lightweight & Mobile Ready

Built to load instantaneously on campus Wi-Fi or mobile data so you can pull up a formula sheet walking into class.

LATENCY: STATIC FAST
04

Peer-Maintained

Continuously audited by our 12-person student team. Whenever faculty upload new revisions, the index stays in sync.
